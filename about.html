About AuctionDesk

AuctionDesk is an automotive marketing and merchandising service operated by CWB Venture, LLC, based in Wisconsin. We work with car dealers, repair shops, body shops, detailers, and tire shops across the US.

Start with the problem, not a package

A lot of automotive marketing is sold as a bundle: a website, some ads, a dashboard, a long contract. Small dealers and shops end up paying for pieces they don't need while the real problem goes unsolved.

We work the other way around. Tell us what's slowing your business down, and we'll scope the work that fixes it. Sometimes that's a full plan. Sometimes it's one thing done well.

Where we work. We're based in Wisconsin. SEO, websites, ads, social media, reviews, and listing copy are done remotely for automotive businesses anywhere in the US.

On-site work, like vehicle photography, is available in Wisconsin.

How we work

A few things we hold ourselves to.

  • You approve everything. Nothing goes live under your business's name without your sign-off.
  • Month-to-month. 30 days' written notice on either side. No long-term contract.
  • Plain-English reporting. What changed, what we did, and what's next, every month.
  • Your accounts stay yours. We work inside your Google, ad, and social accounts.
  • Honest about limits. No guaranteed rankings, fake reviews, or shortcuts that put your business at risk.

Tell us what's slowing your business down.

A short message is enough. We'll follow up with questions and a plan scoped to your business, with no long-term contract.
