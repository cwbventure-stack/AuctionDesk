Automotive SEO and Google Business Profile management

When someone nearby searches for a used truck, a brake job, or a collision repair, the businesses that show up first get the call. We help dealers and shops show up in Google Search and Google Maps for the searches that matter to them.

What's included

The exact mix depends on where you're starting from. These are the pieces we work with.

Google Business Profile

Accurate categories, services, hours, and photos, plus regular posts and answers to customer questions.

On-site SEO

Page titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, and structured data so Google understands what you do and where.

Local listings and citations

Your name, address, and phone number consistent across Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp, and industry directories.

Pages that match searches

Service and inventory pages written around how customers actually search, like a specific repair or vehicle type in your city.

Technical fixes

Speed, mobile usability, broken links, and indexing problems that quietly keep pages out of search results.

Plain-English reporting

Each month: what changed, what we did, and what's next. No vanity dashboards.

SEO for dealers vs. SEO for shops

Same fundamentals, different priorities.

For car dealers

Your inventory changes every week, so search visibility has to keep up.

  • Inventory and vehicle pages Google can find and index
  • Vehicle structured data on detail pages
  • Pages for the makes, models, and price ranges people search
  • A Google Business Profile that reflects your lot

For repair, body, and service shops

Most of your customers find you on Google Maps, often on a phone, often in a hurry.

  • A dedicated page for each service you offer
  • Clear service-area coverage for nearby towns
  • Review signals that help you rank in the map results
  • Hours, photos, and services kept accurate

What we won't do

Some SEO shortcuts work for a few months and then get a business penalized. We don't use them.

  • No guaranteed rankings. Google decides those.
  • No buying links or joining link schemes.
  • No fake reviews or fake listings.
  • No locking you into our accounts. Your profile stays yours.
