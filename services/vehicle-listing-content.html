Vehicle photography and listing copy for dealers

Most listings are the same three sentences with the trim level swapped out. A buyer scrolling past forty of them can't tell your car apart from the one two rows down. We write from a VIN decode and what's actually on the vehicle, so the listing describes the car in front of you.

Get In Touch How it works

What's included

Copy is done remotely, nationwide. Photography is on-site in Wisconsin.

Listing copy

Written from the VIN decode plus the options and features actually on the vehicle, not the trim's spec sheet.

Condition notes

Wear, damage, and recent work called out plainly, so buyers trust the rest of the listing.

Consistent photo sets

30 to 40 images in a fixed angle sequence: exterior, interior, dash with odometer, engine bay, trunk, and any damage.

Clean lead photos

The first photo gets a clean background so the vehicle stands out in search results.

Draft approval

Every listing comes to you as a draft. Nothing publishes until you sign off.

Upload

Loaded into your inventory system or website where your platform allows it.

Remote or on-site

Pick what fits your lot.

Remote, anywhere in the US

For dealers who already take their own photos.

  • Listing copy from your VINs, photos, and notes
  • Lead-photo cleanup
  • Condition notes and feature callouts
  • Drafts delivered for your approval

On-site, in Wisconsin

For dealers who want the whole job handled on the lot.

  • Full photo sets shot on your lot
  • Everything in the remote service
  • Scheduled visits that fit your inventory flow
  • Vehicles just need to be washed and ready

Not included

  • Reconditioning, detailing, or mechanical work. Vehicles need to be clean before photos.
  • Pricing or appraisal advice. That's your call.
  • Certifying a vehicle's condition. We describe what we see; you verify and approve it.
  • Facebook Marketplace workarounds. They break Meta's rules and risk your account.
