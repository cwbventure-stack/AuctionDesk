Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

AuctionDesk is operated by CWB Venture, LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("we," "us"). This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

The only personal information we collect is what you choose to submit through the contact form: your business name, your name, your email address, and optionally a phone number, your type of business, and any message you write. We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies on this site, and we don't collect information automatically beyond what your browser sends to load the page.

How we use it

We use the information you submit only to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to set up and administer your service agreement. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers described below.

Third-party services we use

  • Google Fonts. This site loads typefaces from Google's servers. Google may log the request, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Form delivery service. When you submit the contact form, it's delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service so we can read it by email. That provider processes the message only to route it to us.
  • Hosting. This site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to operate and secure the service.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you or, if you sign a service agreement with us, for the duration of that relationship and any retention period that agreement requires.

Your rights

You can ask what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send your request through the contact form.

Children's privacy

This is a business-to-business service for automotive businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and this site isn't directed at them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
