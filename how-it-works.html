How working with AuctionDesk works

Every engagement runs the same six steps, whatever we're solving. You always know what's happening, and nothing goes live under your name without your approval.

The six steps

  1. 01

    Tell us the problem

    Rankings, your website, ads, reviews, listings. Whatever is actually slowing you down.

  2. 02

    We scope a plan

    A written plan built around your business, not a one-size-fits-all package.

  3. 03

    We get to work

    SEO, web, ads, reviews, listing content. Whatever the plan calls for.

  4. 04

    You review everything

    Nothing goes live without your sign-off first.

  5. 05

    It goes live

    Delivered wherever it needs to land: your site, your listings, your profiles.

  6. 06

    We stick around

    Month-to-month, so the plan changes as your business does.

Each step, in detail

  1. STEP 01

    Tell us the problem

    Send a short message or fill out the form. Tell us what's not working and what you've already tried. You don't need to know which service you need; that's our job to figure out.

  2. STEP 02

    We scope a plan

    We look at your website, Google Business Profile, reviews, listings, and ads, then send a written scope: what we'll do, in what order, what it costs, and when it starts.

  3. STEP 03

    We get to work

    You get one point of contact who knows your business. We handle the work and only pull you in when we need access, information, or a decision.

  4. STEP 04

    You review everything

    Website pages, ad copy, listing descriptions, review responses: you see it and approve it before it's published under your name.

  5. STEP 05

    It goes live

    We publish to your accounts, not ours. Your website, ad accounts, and profiles stay in your name.

  6. STEP 06

    We stick around

    You get a plain-English report each month: what changed, what we did, and what's next. Stay as long as it's working, with 30 days' written notice to stop.

Not included

  • Hands-on vehicle work. No reconditioning, detailing, or mechanical repairs. We market the businesses that do it.
  • Pricing or appraisal advice. What you charge is your call.
  • Taking title to vehicles. We're a service business, not a dealer.
  • Shortcuts that backfire. No fake reviews, bought links, guaranteed rankings, or Facebook Marketplace workarounds.
