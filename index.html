The Wisconsin Angle

Wisconsin doesn't use the federal Buyer's Guide.

Wisconsin is one of only two states the FTC's Used Car Rule doesn't apply to. Instead, dealers here complete the state's own MV2872 Buyer's Guide, based on an actual visual inspection and test drive. A vendor printing the federal form isn't giving you the document Wisconsin requires.

  • Engine size, gauges, and warning devices
  • Oil consumption, fluid seepage, airbag status
  • Title brands, repaired damage, and recall status
  • Warranty terms, with expiration and mileage

Who's responsible for the disclosure? You are. That's the standard — and it's correct.

We collect and record inspection findings on site. You review, verify, and sign every guide. We never certify a vehicle's condition. All listing copy is delivered as a draft for your approval before publishing, since dealer advertising is regulated under Wisconsin law.

What this service doesn't do

  • We don't post to Facebook Marketplace. Meta banned direct dealer posting in January 2023. Workarounds violate their terms and risk your account. We won't sell something that doesn't work.

  • We don't advise on pricing or appraisals. That's your decision. We stay out of it.
  • We never take title to a vehicle. We're a service business, not a dealer.
